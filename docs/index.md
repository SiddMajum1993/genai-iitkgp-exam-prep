## How this site is organised

The same 12 lectures appear in three depths so you can pick the one that matches your time and energy today:

- **[Learn](learn/index.md)** — 800–2,000 lines per lecture. Plain-English concept walkthroughs. This is the primary reading.
- **[Revise](revise/index.md)** — 200–400 lines per lecture. Formulas, tables, flashcards. Great once you already understand it.
- **[Practice](practice/index.md)** — five interactive mock exams with score tracking, per-section breakdowns, and full answer explanations.
- **[Cheat sheet](cheatsheet.md)** — one page total. For the last hour before the exam.
