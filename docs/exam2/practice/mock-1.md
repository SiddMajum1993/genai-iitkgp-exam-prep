# Mock Exam 1 · Official practice set (part 1 of 4)

Part 1 of the **official practice set** provided by IIT Kharagpur, split into four 24-question papers. This part has 23 of its questions, spread evenly across its three blocks: 5 fine-tuning, 8 VLM and multimodal RAG, and 10 agents. One extra fine-tuning question, marked **(Added question)**, brings it to 24.

!!! note "What we changed"
    The question wording is the official one. We **shuffled the option order**, because in the original the right answer was usually B (and never D in the agents block, never A in the fine-tuning block). We also wrote the explanations: the original only had them for the VLM block.

    One pattern we couldn't remove: in the original, **the correct answer is usually the longest option**. Don't rely on that in the real exam. Mocks 5–8 don't have it.

Set aside 90 uninterrupted minutes, keep scratch paper handy and answer every question. There's no negative marking.

!!! tip "How the interactive quiz works"
    - The timer starts when you answer your first question.
    - You can change any answer until you submit.
    - Submitting shows your score, a breakdown by section and an explanation for every question, including why each wrong option is wrong.
    - You can reset and retake it as many times as you like.
