# About this site

## What this is

A study companion for the **upGrad × IIT Kharagpur *Certificate Course on Generative and Agentic AI***. The course covers 12 lectures on modern LLMs, RAG systems, agentic AI, and safety, culminating in a proctored MCQ exam.

This site distils those 12 lectures into three depths — deep learn-guides, compressed revision notes, and a one-page cheat sheet — plus five full mock exams for practice.

## What this is **not**

- Not an official upGrad or IIT Kharagpur publication
- Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the course faculty
- Not a substitute for attending the lectures — the guides *summarise and reframe*; they don't replace the original teaching

## About the author
